# Aravalli environment study — draft, not approved

Input: four Copernicus GLO-90 2021-release DSM tiles, N25/N26 E073/E074, covering approximately 73–75° E and 25–27° N. It is a surface model including buildings and vegetation, not a detailed terrain survey or land-cover map. Source and license: https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/ ; credit: “produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-90 © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved”. Source cells are nominal 90 m; the 300 × 300 resampled output is roughly 0.006686° between samples (approximately 740 m north/south and 670 m east/west). It cannot resolve small details. Raw tiles remain cached and ignored.

The TIFF GeoKey 1025 reports RasterPixelIsPoint; the stored tiepoint is the first sample center. The new grid transform records the first center and output spacing separately from the source resolution. A check compared the northwest original and resampled sample (295.5575 m) and confirmed positive east and negative north-to-south steps. This is a limited check, not independent surveyed ground control. The projection remains the existing local spherical equirectangular approximation; a suitable regional geodetic projection and independent full alignment checks are still required. Older NPZ mosaics do not contain the new transform and must be regenerated before using the updated builder.

The environment study is an isolated 90,000-vertex, 89,401-face mesh with true-scale elevation and original procedural color and bump nodes. No vegetation, water, roads, external textures, HDRI or land-cover data were imported. The procedural variation is artistic, not evidence of actual surface cover. The render is 960 × 600, 16 Cycles CPU samples and took approximately 5.14 seconds using 8 Blender threads. The result does not meet the requested visual realism, haze, color or composition. Blender 5.1.1 Flatpak still falls back to disabled color management because its OpenColorIO configuration is 2.5 and runtime 2.4.2. Prior verification showed Eevee works headlessly but CUDA did not detect the RTX 5060, and OptiX was unavailable. An official Blender Linux archive can run alongside system installation according to https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/getting_started/installing/linux.html ; it was not downloaded or installed.

Rebuild: `python3 artifacts/rajasthan-world/scripts/mosaic_terrain.py --bbox 73 25 75 27 --samples-per-degree 150 --output artifacts/rajasthan-world/data/aravalli-study.npz` then `flatpak run --filesystem=/home/adityadugar/Documents/Projects/Portfolio-experiment org.blender.Blender --background --factory-startup --threads 8 --python /home/adityadugar/Documents/Projects/Portfolio-experiment/artifacts/rajasthan-world/scripts/build_environment_study.py`.

No Rajasthan boundary or fort coordinates have been independently validated and no statewide master was created. No OSM or Sentinel-2 data have been acquired. Outputs A–F other than this single draft, the 1440 × 900 hero, Bikaner study and viewport screenshot remain outstanding.
