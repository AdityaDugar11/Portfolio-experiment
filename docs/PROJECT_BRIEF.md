## Working Title & Core Concept

**THE LIVING SIGNAL**

A complete recreation and redevelopment of Aditya's portfolio into a world-class, cinematic personal narrative that combines:
- Premium technology product design.
- Real-time Three.js/WebGL 3D environments.
- Cinematic camera choreography and scene transitions.
- Scroll-controlled storytelling (inspired in creative quality by [Ameen Abdullah](https://ameen-abdullah.dev/), with an original identity).
- Realistic human portrait integration (no low-poly, pixel-art, or voxel shortcuts).
- Expressive editorial typography and custom dot-and-ring cursor micro-interactions.
- Subtle cultural & architectural resonance (sandstone haveli framing, lakeside palace vistas, warm sunset atmosphere).

## Scope & Structure

The finished website encompasses all **ten chapters** documented in `MASTER_CREATIVE_BRIEF.md`, organized around **four major cinematic sequences**:

1. **Sequence 01 — The Introduction (Chapter 01)**:
   Identity, sunset lakeside atmosphere, haveli archway with bougainvillea, realistic portrait, floating holographic technical elements, and 5-stage orbital transformation into connected technical topics.
2. **Sequence 02 — From Curiosity to Connected Systems (Chapters 02–05)**:
   - *Chapter 02: Beyond the Interface* (surfaces transforming into digital system layers).
   - *Chapter 03: The Learning Curve* (spatial exploration journey of fundamentals and debugging).
   - *Chapter 04: What Draws My Attention* (3D interconnected dimensional graphics of 6 core domains).
   - *Chapter 05: How I Turn Ideas into Systems* (software architecture diagrams and connected tech nodes).
3. **Sequence 03 — The Project Gallery & Collaboration (Chapters 06–07)**:
   - *Chapter 06: The Work I Choose to Show* (live dynamic GitHub GraphQL pinned repositories gallery).
   - *Chapter 07: Engineering with Others* (authentic GrooveIt collaborative product engineering narrative).
4. **Sequence 04 — The Future and Closing (Chapters 08–10)**:
   - *Chapter 08: The Experimental Lab* (floating 3D experiments, local models, agents, Linux).
   - *Chapter 09: Still Becoming* (spacious cinematic camera journey into expanding frontiers).
   - *Chapter 10: The Next Connection* (closing visual synthesis, accessible contact modal, and links).
