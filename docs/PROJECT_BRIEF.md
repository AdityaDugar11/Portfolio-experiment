## Working title

**THE LIVING SIGNAL**

A personal portfolio presented as a connected, cinematic journey through curiosity, learning, engineering experiments, projects, collaboration and future direction.

## Primary goal

Build a memorable portfolio that combines an honest personal narrative with a distinctive interactive visual experience. Visitors should understand who Aditya is, what technical questions interest him, how he learns and approaches problems, what he has actually built, what he contributed during his GrooveIt internship, what he wants to explore next, and how to contact him.

## Audience

Recruiters, engineers, collaborators, peers, and people interested in software engineering, AI, systems, automation and creative technology.

## Personality and voice

- Curious and thoughtful, with genuine technical interests.
- Personal without being overly sentimental.
- Confident but honest about current skill level.
- Clear and specific rather than buzzword-heavy.
- A learning journey, not a false claim of mastery.

Avoid inflated claims such as “expert”, “industry-leading” or “revolutionary” unless the owner later supplies evidence and approves them.

## Experience principles

1. One connected story, not unrelated website sections.
2. Each chapter has a distinct visual concept while preserving the overall visual language.
3. Scroll drives purposeful transformations, not animation for its own sake.
4. Content remains readable and accessible.
5. The site performs on ordinary devices, not only the owner's gaming laptop.
6. Visual ambition must not compromise navigation, usability or performance.

## Scope

The story has ten chapters. Build and approve Act 01 first. Do not implement the other chapters until Act 01 is approved.
