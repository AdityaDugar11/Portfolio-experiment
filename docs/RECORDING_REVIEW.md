# Review of Failed Act 01 Recording — 4 November 2026

This review is based on the approximately 11-second screen recording supplied by the owner after the latest attempted implementation.

## What is visible in the recording

- The opening view has a small editorial-style name/quote layout and a cutout portrait on a very pale background.
- During scroll, the text fades and a few thin peach/orange loops appear around the portrait.
- Topic cards appear around the portrait, but their positions and connector paths look loosely arranged rather than forming one coherent orbital composition.
- The background reads mostly as a flat pale surface. It does not communicate the expansive peach-and-blue cloud environment in the approved storyboard.
- The portrait remains visually like a cutout with a lower fade/edge; it does not feel naturally integrated into a scene with atmospheric depth.
- The final composition is more like a sparse diagram around a portrait than the cinematic five-stage storyboard.
- At one point while reversing/transitioning, introductory text appears faintly behind the portrait, suggesting the outgoing intro layer is not being fully hidden/managed cleanly.

## Priority corrections

1. **Environment first:** introduce a convincing peach/soft-blue cloud sky with depth, light and atmospheric layering. A flat cream gradient is not enough.
2. **Recompose the orbital system:** create a deliberate set of differently oriented, overlapping elliptical/curved paths around the portrait. They must look like a designed system, not random scribbles.
3. **Scroll-driven formation:** reveal each ring progressively from its path start to end. Stage this over scroll progress. Do not have every loop present immediately.
4. **Connectors must be meaningful:** each topic card should attach to a clear endpoint on the orbital system. Show the connection drawing before or as the card appears.
5. **Card hierarchy:** reveal five cards in a deliberate sequence and place them around the portrait with balanced spacing. Increase readability and avoid generic dashboard styling.
6. **Portrait fit:** preserve the complete portrait and scale it to fit the final composition. Do not zoom so far that the body is lost.
7. **Layer cleanup:** ensure intro text is completely hidden after its transition; no ghosting behind the final scene.
8. **Atmospheric integration:** use subtle light, depth and parallax to integrate the portrait, background, rings and cards. Do not add a grey panel, pedestal or giant shadow.

## Validation required

Capture actual browser states at 0%, 20%, 40%, 60%, 85% and 100%, then scroll back to 0%. The owner must be able to distinguish the stages and see the complete portrait and all five connected cards in the final state.
